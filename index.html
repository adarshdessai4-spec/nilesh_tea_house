About Us

Since ancient times, chai has been a part of everyday life across Asia. Nilesh Tea House brings that timeless tradition into today’s world with fresh, aromatic teas and indulgent milkshakes.

Our story began in 1940, when our grandfather opened a humble tea stall with a big dream. Today, as a husband–wife team, we’re proudly carrying that legacy forward—serving chai that tastes like home, made with love, simplicity and family warmth.

From classic masala chai to modern coolers, every drink is crafted to match your mood and moment, with quality ingredients, consistent taste and a cozy neighborhood café feel.

One cup at a time, we’re keeping a 1940 ki chai ki kahani alive. 💛☕
